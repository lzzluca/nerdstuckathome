## Where I came from

I started working in 2008 in Italy, spent about three years there, then three in London, and I've been in the Netherlands since.

My longest stretch so far was six years at EclecticIQ. Software security had always fascinated me, and when the chance came to join a company in that space I took it. I joined as a senior UI developer: senior by years of experience, but quite lost about how my role should evolve. I thought the next step had to be leadership, without a clear picture of what leadership even was. Mostly, I wanted to work on a product that excited me.

Until then I'd been the coder who wants to code in isolation, hiding in his corner, staying as far as possible from team dynamics, management and the company around me.

## Personal life

I like life in the Netherlands, but it doesn't come problem-free: buying a flat is close to impossible with prices that keep growing, and friends come and go. I also go back to Italy often to see family and friends. It feels right, but it splits my life in two. I'm keeping the door open to a future in both countries, with the uncertainty that comes with it. I'd like to decide where to settle down; I guess that's a common problem for many people who live abroad.

For the record, home in Italy is [Bellaria Igea Marina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellaria-Igea_Marina), in the province of Rimini, on the northern Adriatic coast: a cheerful seaside town of around 20,000 people.
