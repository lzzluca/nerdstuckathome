I'm starting a series of 30-day challenges, and I'm writing them here so I can't forget about them. The rules are written before I start, and the progress gets logged as I go, including the days it doesn't work.

## The current challenge

The first one is about screens. For the next 30 days:

- No TV.
- No Netflix.
- No YouTube. Videos that matter reach me through my [weekly digest](/posts/self-hosted-rss-pipeline) as summaries, and YouTube is only a player I open from a link, never a place to browse.
- No internet at home: I pretend the house has no connection and only the phone does.

And what fills the time instead:

- Reading.
- Podcasts.
